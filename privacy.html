Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 · Applies to Door Count for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS

Short version: Door Count does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal or financial data. We operate no backend servers, require no user account, connect to no banks, and embed zero analytics or advertising SDKs.

1. Data We Collect: None

Andrew Virts ("we", "us", or "the developer") does not collect any personal information, property addresses, tenant contact information, rent ledgers, tax figures, receipt images, location data, or usage telemetry from Door Count.

2. Where Your Data Lives

  • On-Device Storage: By default, all properties, units, leases, rent payments, expenses, mileage logs, deposit deductions, and receipt attachments you enter are stored locally on your Apple device inside Door Count's sandboxed SwiftData container (shared only with Door Count's own widgets and share extension on your device via Apple App Groups).
  • Optional Private iCloud Sync (CloudKit): If you enable Private iCloud Sync in Settings (available during the free trial and with Door Count Pro), your records sync directly between your own Apple devices through Apple's private CloudKit database tied to your Apple Account. The developer cannot access, read, or decrypt your private iCloud container, and your iCloud storage is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • Camera & Imported Files (Receipt OCR): When you scan a receipt or import an image/PDF invoice, text recognition runs 100% locally on your device using Apple's built-in Vision and PDFKit frameworks. Images and extracted text are never uploaded to any external OCR or AI server.
  • Local Notifications: If enabled, reminders (lease notice and security deposit return dates, rent, recurring bills, reminders you add, and one reminder before your free trial ends) are scheduled locally on your device using Apple's UserNotifications framework. No push notification server or device token is sent to us.
  • Dictation & Siri Shortcuts: Any spoken quick-log phrase is processed by Apple's system dictation/Siri infrastructure and parsed locally on your device.

4. Purchases & Billing

Door Count Pro (a one-time purchase) and the free 30-day trial are processed exclusively by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your credit card, Apple Pay, or billing details. Apple provides the app only with a cryptographic entitlement receipt on your device confirming whether the trial has started and whether Door Count Pro is unlocked.

5. Exports & Sharing

When you export a CSV file, PDF statement, CPA Tax Pack (.zip), or backup (.doorcountbackup), the file is generated locally on your device and shared only with the destination you explicitly choose in the system share sheet or Finder.

6. Deleting Your Data

You have complete control over your records at all times:

  • You can delete individual properties, payments, expenses, deductions, or mileage trips inside the app, or use Settings → Delete All Data. With iCloud Sync on, that also deletes them from your iCloud and your other devices. Settings → Backup & Restore saves everything to a file you keep first.
  • Uninstalling Door Count removes local app data from that device.
  • If you enabled Private iCloud Sync, you can manage or delete Door Count's iCloud storage anytime in Apple's system settings under Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

7. Children's Privacy

Door Count is a general-audience bookkeeping utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact

If Door Count's privacy practices ever change, we will update this page and revise the Effective Date above. Questions about privacy can be sent directly to asvirts@outlook.com.
