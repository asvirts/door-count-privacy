Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Door Count Pro on my other Apple devices?

Door Count Pro is a one-time Apple Universal Purchase. Ensure you are signed into the same Apple Account on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, open Door Count → Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. You will never be charged twice.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Nothing is deleted and you are never charged automatically. Your records stay on your devices, and you can still view them, export the CPA Tax Pack and PDFs, and back up or restore them. Adding or changing records is paused until you buy Door Count Pro, a one-time purchase, from Settings or the banner on the Rent tab.

If reminders are on, Door Count sends you one notification at 9 AM three days before your trial ends. To turn it off, open Settings → Reminders and switch off Free Trial Ending.

How do I redeem an offer code?

Open Door Count → Settings and tap Redeem Offer Code, or tap Redeem Code at the bottom of the purchase screen, then enter the code. A Door Count Pro code unlocks the app on every device signed in to the same Apple Account, just like buying it.

How do I turn on Private iCloud Sync?

Open Settings inside Door Count (⌘, on Mac or the gear icon on iPhone/iPad) and turn on iCloud Sync. Use the same Apple Account on every device, then close and reopen Door Count once on each device so it connects to your private iCloud.

How do I back up my records or move them to a new device?

Open Settings → Backup & Restore and tap Back Up All Data. Door Count saves every property, lease, payment, expense, mileage trip and receipt to one .doorcountbackup file wherever you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. To restore, tap Restore from Backup (or open the file). Door Count first shows what the backup holds, then lets you replace all data with it or add only the records that are missing. Backup and restore always work, even after the free trial ends. The file is plain JSON, so your records are never locked in.

Does Door Count connect to my bank via Plaid or message my tenants?

No—by design. Door Count never asks for bank credentials and never contacts your tenants. When a Zelle, Venmo, ACH, or check arrives, tap Paid on the Rent Board in one second.

Are refundable security deposits included in Schedule E Line 3 income?

No. Security deposits held on a lease are excluded from Schedule E Line 3 (Rents received). Only amounts you explicitly mark to post to Schedule E income during a move-out deposit settlement are added to Line 3.

How does Owner-Occupied ("House-Hack") proration work?

When adding a property (e.g., a duplex where you live in one unit and rent the other), enable Owner-Occupied ('House-Hack' % Split) and set your rental business-use percentage. Expenses logged to Whole Building (Shared) automatically prorate by that percentage on your Schedule E report, while expenses logged to a specific rented unit remain 100% allocated to that unit.

Why are credit card expenses excluded from the 1099-NEC tracker?

Under IRS Form 1099-NEC instructions, payments made by credit/debit card or third-party settlement networks are reported by the payment processor on Form 1099-K rather than by the landlord on Form 1099-NEC. Door Count tracks the 1099-NEC threshold for the tax year ($600 for 2025 payments, $2,000 for payments made in 2026 and later) across check, Zelle, ACH, cash, and personal transfer payments for services such as repairs, cleaning, and management, while keeping card expenses separate. Always confirm filing requirements with your CPA.
